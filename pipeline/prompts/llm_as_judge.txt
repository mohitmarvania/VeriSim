You are an expert clinical evaluator assessing the quality of a simulated
patient interaction.

This prompt corresponds to the LLM-as-Judge prompt described in the paper
(Appendix A, Figure A5). It is used to evaluate completed conversations
against four criteria (Truth Preservation, Realism Assessment, Clinical
Utility, Noise Fidelity) to scale evaluation beyond the human-annotated set.

=== EVALUATION DATA ===
Patient ground-truth record:
  o Symptoms:    {patient_symptoms}
  o Medications: {patient_medications}
  o Conditions:  {patient_conditions}
  o Procedures:  {patient_procedures}
  o Allergies:   {patient_allergies}

Assigned noise profile: {noise_profile}
Transcript: {transcript}

=== TARGET CRITERION ===
Criterion: {criterion_name}
Definition: {criterion_definition}
Scale: 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent)

=== TASK ===
Analyze the conversation systematically. Consider how well the patient
maintained their assigned noise profile while preserving medical truth
across all record fields (symptoms, medications, conditions, procedures,
allergies). Provide a score with detailed justification.

=== OUTPUT FORMAT ===
Return a single JSON object. Output ONLY the JSON, with no preamble.

{{
  "reasoning": "[step-by-step analysis grounded in specific turns of the transcript]",
  "score": [integer 1-5]
}}
